How to Choose a Freelance Platform

Different platforms work better for different skills and experience levels. Consider the type of services you offer, the clients you want to reach, platform fees, competition and payment options.

Before accepting work, understand the platform's rules, fees and payment process. Never pay someone simply because they promise to give you a freelance job.
