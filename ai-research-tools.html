How to Choose an AI Research Tool

Choose a research tool based on the type of information you need. General AI assistants can help with everyday research, while specialized research tools may be more useful for academic papers and evidence-based research.

Always check important information against reliable original sources before using it in professional, academic or business work.

Research Smarter With AI

AI can make research faster and easier, but responsible research still requires checking important information and using trustworthy sources.
