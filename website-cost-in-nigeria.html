How Much Does It Cost to Build a Website in Nigeria?

If you are planning to build a website in Nigeria, one of the first questions you may have is: How much will it cost?

The answer depends on the type of website you want, the features you need, who builds it, the platform you use, and the services you choose.

A simple landing page is very different from a professional business website, an online store, or a custom web application. That is why website prices in Nigeria can vary widely.

If you want a general explanation of website pricing beyond Nigeria, you can also read our guide: How Much Does a Website Cost?

How Much Does a Website Cost in Nigeria in 2026?

There is no single official price for building a website in Nigeria. Current 2026 market pricing guides show that the cost can range from tens of thousands of naira for very simple projects to several millions of naira for complex websites and web applications.

For practical planning, the following are useful indicative ranges. They are not fixed national prices. Your actual quotation may be lower or higher depending on the scope, developer, agency, platform, design, features and other requirements.

Typical Website Cost Ranges in Nigeria

  • Landing page: ₦80,000 – ₦400,000
  • Basic business website: ₦150,000 – ₦500,000
  • Professional custom website: ₦500,000 – ₦2,500,000+
  • E-commerce website: ₦400,000 – ₦3,500,000+
  • Custom web application: ₦1,500,000 – ₦10,000,000+

These figures should be used as planning ranges rather than promises. Different Nigerian developers and agencies may quote very different prices for projects that sound similar because the actual scope can be very different.

What Determines the Cost of a Website in Nigeria?

Several factors can affect the amount you pay for a website.

  • Number of pages
  • Website design and branding
  • Development technology
  • E-commerce functionality
  • Online payment integration
  • Booking systems
  • Customer accounts
  • Content creation
  • Search engine optimization
  • Maintenance and technical support

1. Domain Name

A domain name is the web address people use to find your website, such as:

yourbusiness.com

Domain prices depend on the extension and the registrar you choose. Common extensions include .com, .org, .net and Nigerian extensions such as .ng and .com.ng.

Domain registration is normally renewed periodically, so do not look only at the first-year promotional price. Check the renewal price as well.

2. Web Hosting

Web hosting stores your website files and makes them available to visitors on the internet.

For Nigerian website owners, hosting costs can vary considerably depending on whether you choose shared hosting, managed WordPress hosting, VPS hosting or cloud infrastructure.

Current 2026 Nigerian hosting guides commonly place basic shared hosting in the broad range of roughly ₦20,000–₦120,000 per year, although prices vary by provider and plan. Promotional prices and renewal prices can also be very different. 1

3. Website Design

Website design determines how your website looks and how visitors interact with it.

A simple template-based website can cost less than a completely custom design created around a company's branding.

Professional design may include custom layouts, colours, typography, graphics, mobile responsiveness, user experience planning and other visual work.

4. Website Development

Development involves building the website and making its features work.

A basic informational website may require only a few pages, while a more advanced website may require databases, user accounts, payment systems, dashboards or other custom functionality.

If you already know HTML, CSS and JavaScript, you can build many simple websites yourself and reduce some development costs.

5. Website Features

Additional features can increase the total project cost.

Examples include:

  • Online payment systems
  • Shopping carts
  • Customer accounts
  • Booking systems
  • Contact forms
  • Search functionality
  • Membership systems
  • Custom dashboards
  • Third-party integrations

6. E-commerce Website Costs

An e-commerce website normally costs more than a simple informational website because it may require product catalogues, shopping carts, customer accounts, payment processing, order management and delivery features.

Current Nigerian pricing guides place many e-commerce projects in the hundreds of thousands to several millions of naira, depending heavily on the size and functionality of the store. 2

A small store with a limited product catalogue is very different from a large store with thousands of products, inventory management and automated integrations.

7. Website Content

Your website also needs useful content.

This can include:

  • Written content
  • Product descriptions
  • Professional photographs
  • Graphics
  • Videos
  • SEO content

Creating your own content can reduce the initial budget, while hiring writers, photographers, designers or other professionals can increase the overall cost.

8. Website Maintenance

Building a website is not always the end of the expense.

Websites may require ongoing maintenance such as:

  • Domain renewal
  • Hosting renewal
  • Security updates
  • Content updates
  • Backups
  • Technical support

Some website owners handle these tasks themselves, while others pay a developer or maintenance provider.

Example Website Budgets in Nigeria

Instead of asking only, "How much does a website cost?", it is better to ask what type of website you actually need.

Example 1: Small Business Website

A small business may need a homepage, about page, services page, gallery and contact page.

A reasonable planning range for this type of project could be around ₦150,000–₦500,000, depending on design, platform, content and the person or company building it.

Example 2: Professional Custom Website

A growing company may need custom branding, additional pages, a blog, forms, SEO setup and other business features.

A project of this type can move into the ₦500,000–₦2,500,000+ range, depending on its scope.

Example 3: E-commerce Website

An online store may require products, categories, shopping carts, payments, customer accounts, delivery options and order management.

A reasonable planning range can be around ₦400,000–₦3,500,000+, although a very simple store can cost less and a complex store can cost considerably more.

Example 4: Custom Web Application

A web application may include dashboards, user accounts, permissions, databases, payments, booking systems or other custom business logic.

These projects can start around ₦1,500,000 and can reach many millions of naira as complexity increases.

Can You Build a Website With a Small Budget?

Yes. Beginners can reduce their initial costs by learning to build the website themselves and starting with only the features they actually need.

You can begin with a simple website and add more features as your business grows.

This can be especially useful when you are testing a new business idea and do not want to spend heavily before knowing whether the idea works.

How to Reduce Website Costs

Here are practical ways to control your website budget:

  • Start with only the pages you need
  • Compare domain and hosting providers
  • Learn basic web development
  • Use suitable website templates when appropriate
  • Create some content yourself
  • Avoid paying for features you do not need
  • Check renewal prices before purchasing services
  • Ask developers for a clear list of what their quotation includes

What Should You Ask a Web Developer Before Paying?

If you are hiring a Nigerian developer or agency, do not compare quotations based only on the final price.

Ask what is included in the quotation.

  • How many pages are included?
  • Is the domain included?
  • Is hosting included?
  • Is the website mobile-friendly?
  • Who provides the content?
  • Are payment integrations included?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • Who owns the website and domain?
  • What will renewal and maintenance cost?

A very cheap quotation may not include services that another developer has already included. Comparing the actual deliverables is therefore more useful than comparing numbers alone.

Final Thoughts

The cost of building a website in Nigeria depends heavily on what you are trying to build.

A simple website can be started with a relatively small budget, while a professional e-commerce website or custom web application can require a much larger investment.

Before spending money, define what your website needs to accomplish. Then compare your options, understand what each quotation includes, and start with the features that matter most.

Website prices also change over time, so treat the figures in this guide as planning ranges rather than permanent prices.
