How to Create Your First Website Online

Creating your first website may look difficult at first, but you can start with simple tools and gradually learn the skills you need.

1. Decide What Your Website Is About

Before writing any code, decide what your website will be used for. It could be a business website, personal portfolio, blog, educational website or online store.

2. Plan Your Website

Decide which pages you need. A simple website may begin with Home, About, Services, Guides and Contact pages.

3. Learn Basic HTML

HTML provides the structure of a website. You can use HTML to create headings, paragraphs, links, images, buttons and other parts of your pages.

4. Learn Basic CSS

CSS controls how your website looks. It can be used to control colours, spacing, fonts, buttons, layouts and mobile responsiveness.

5. Build Your Website

Create your HTML pages and connect them together using links. Keep your website simple at first and improve it as you learn.

6. Test Your Website

Open your website in a browser and test every page. Check your navigation links, buttons, text and layout. Also test the website on your phone because many visitors will use mobile devices.

7. Get a Domain Name and Hosting

When your website is ready to go online, you will need a domain name and web hosting. The domain is your website address, while hosting stores your website files online.

8. Publish Your Website

Upload your website files to your hosting account and connect your domain name. Once everything is configured, people can access your website through your domain.

Start Small and Keep Learning

You do not need to build a complicated website on your first attempt. Start with a simple project, learn from your mistakes and gradually add new features.
